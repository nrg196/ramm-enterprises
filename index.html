About Ramm Enterprises

Established in 1995 in Peelamedu, Coimbatore, Ramm Enterprises manufactures, supplies and trades spinning ancillary machines for the textile industry. Our aim is to be our customers' technology partner of choice.

Our range covers roll shop machinery, cot mounting and buffing machines, card room grinding and wire mounting equipment, drafting and ring frame accessories, gauges, tools and mill consumables.

Our team of quality controllers, logistics staff and sales specialists is trained regularly. We follow 7S and ISO systems across manufacturing and sales, guided by our quality goal: "Manufacture to Edge of Quality in Need."
